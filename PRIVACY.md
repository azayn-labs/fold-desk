# FoldDesk privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

FoldDesk is a home screen (launcher) for foldable Android phones, published by
Azayn. This policy covers both the free version on GitHub and the version on
Google Play.

## The short version

FoldDesk does not collect, store off the device, sell or share any personal
data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What FoldDesk uses on your phone, and why

Everything below stays on your phone and is only used to show it to you.

- **Installed apps**, to list them in the app drawer, taskbar and dock.
- **Accessibility service** (the FoldDesk taskbar), only once you turn it on:
  to show the taskbar above other apps, to know which apps and windows are
  open, and to press Back, Home, Recents and the window buttons when you use
  the taskbar. It does not read what you type or the content of other apps.
- **Notifications** (if you allow notification access), to show them on the
  cover screen and open or dismiss them when you ask.
- **Usage access** (if you allow it), to show your recently used apps.
- **Calendar** (if you allow it), to show today's and tomorrow's events on the
  cover screen.
- **Widgets** you add, which show content from their own apps.

Your settings and layout are saved in FoldDesk's private storage on the phone
and are deleted when you uninstall it.

## Purchases

If you buy FoldDesk on Google Play, the payment is handled by Google. FoldDesk
receives no payment or account details from it.
